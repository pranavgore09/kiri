# Brand — working notes (not final)

## Working name: Ira Kiri

### Meaning check
- **ira**: life principle, essence (as in *ira tangata*, "the life of people").
- **kiri**: skin. Leather is skin, so the name fits.
- "Ira Kiri" ≈ "the life of the skin". The story's "life-force, character, and markings" is a loose reading of that. Get a te reo speaker to confirm it before going public.
- Pronunciation: **EE-rah KEE-ree**. English speakers may say "EYE-ra". Put a one-line pronunciation note on the About page.

### Before finalising
- Search for the name on the NZ IPONZ register, the US USPTO register, Instagram, and as a .com / .co domain.
- Te reo used as a brand by a non-Māori maker can draw criticism in NZ. Keep the story honest: say you fell for Aotearoa, rather than claiming any heritage. If you can, ask a te reo speaker to approve the name.
- "Kiri" on its own is a common name (e.g. Kiri Te Kanawa). As two words, "Ira Kiri" is distinct.

### Story (tightened draft)
> **Ira Kiri**: the life of the skin.
> Every piece starts as a blank canvas, cut and stitched by hand to your design. Then it's yours to wear in. Scratches, oils and sun turn into a patina no one else has.
> Not a wallet you replace. One that carries your story.

Original:
> "Ira Kiri" translates from te reo Māori to mean the unique life-force, character, and markings of the leather. We don't make mass-produced, throwaway goods. We create custom, handmade leather pieces that start as a blank canvas and evolve with you. Every scratch, oil, and shadow creates a beautiful patina unique to your journey. It is not just a wallet—it's a companion that carries your story.

## Other name ideas (no alliterative "Gold Grizzly" pairs)
- **Second Skin**: plain English, ties to "kiri"; widely used, so check availability.
- **Worn Story**: matches the patina story.
- **Kiri & Thread**: keeps the Māori word, easier to read.
- **Slow Hide**: handmade, not mass-produced.
